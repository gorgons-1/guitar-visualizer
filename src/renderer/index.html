Tab player

Paste a guitar tab (the text kind, with a line per string like e|---0---3---|) or open a .txt tab file. Each step is shown on the neck, with the next few numbered.

Tip: click any fret to hear that note. When listening, clicking where your hand is helps it pick the right fret.
